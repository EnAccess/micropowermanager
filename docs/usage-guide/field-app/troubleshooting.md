# Troubleshooting

Tap the message you see to find out what it means and what to do.

::: details "Unauthorized. Make sure you are logged in." when signing in
The email or password is wrong, or the account is not an agent account.

Check your details.
Ask your administrator to confirm your agent account or to set a new password.
:::

::: details "Risk balance exceeded"
You are holding as much company cash as you are allowed to.

Hand the money over at headquarters.
Once the [receipt](/usage-guide/agents/charges-and-receipts#agent-receipts) is recorded, you can collect cash again.
:::

::: details "No SHS units assigned to you."
No systems have been assigned to you for sale.

Ask your administrator to [assign appliances](/usage-guide/agents/setting-up-an-agent) to you.
:::

::: details "Villages aren't loaded yet."
The app has not downloaded the villages of your mini-grid.

Connect to the internet once, then try again.
:::

::: details "No device found with serial …"
The serial number is wrong, or the device is not registered in MicroPowerManager.

Check the serial number on the device.
If it is correct, ask your administrator to check the device.
:::

::: details "We could not confirm this payment"
The connection dropped while the payment was being saved, so it may still have gone through.

Check the customer's payments before collecting again, so they do not pay twice.
:::

::: details "The deposit was not paid"
The sale was saved, but the deposit failed.

Do not start a new sale.
Open the sale from **Sales** and collect the deposit again.
:::

::: details SYNC FAILED on a customer
The server rejected a customer you registered offline, for example because the phone number is already registered.

Tap the customer, correct the details shown and save again.
:::
