# Selling solar home systems

1. Tap **Sell SHS** from the orange ":heavy_plus_sign:" button on **Home**.
2. Pick the customer.
3. Pick the system.
   Only the systems your administrator assigned to you are listed.
   If the system needs a serial number, choose the unit you are handing over.
4. Choose how the customer will pay:

   | Plan               | How it works                                                                                                      |
   | ------------------ | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
   | **Cash upfront**   | The customer pays the full price today.                                                                           |
   | **12-month PAYG**  | The customer pays a 10% deposit today and the rest in 12 instalments. Switch to weekly for 12 weekly instalments. |
   | **Custom plan**    | You set the deposit and the number of monthly or weekly instalments.                                              |
   | **Energy service** | The customer pays a daily price through top-ups, with no fixed end date. A down payment is optional.              |

5. If the customer pays the deposit from their own phone, choose the payment method.
6. Check the summary.
   Take the money **before** you tap **Cash received — confirm**; this cannot be undone.

The **Sales** tab lists your sales, filtered by **In payoff** or **Paid off**.
Open a sale to see its plan and payment schedule.
Tap the key icon next to a paid instalment to show its token again.
